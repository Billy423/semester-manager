# Extraction, landing and reading are three concerns and they change for unlike reasons

Extraction changes with the material, landing changes with the schema, and reading changes with agent-engineering practice, so they are three seams rather than one ingestion pipeline. The cost accepted is three seams to maintain; what is rejected is the single pipeline. This is the ruling that retires `ingestion` as a word of this project, and the ruling that makes **candidate fact** a coherent term.
