# Every field and every verb has exactly one purpose

Every schema field and every exposed verb is defined so that its purpose does not overlap another's, in the same way tool definitions must not overlap, because an agent operates from the **definition** rather than from a sense of where a piece of information belongs. The rejected alternative on the table was a negatively defined catch-all free-text field, and its cost was measured across 22 rows: reversing this re-admits it and re-opens the six purposes `obligation.notes` was carrying.
