# A negative answer names the boundary it speaks for; a bare "it is not known" shifts the burden to the reader

**faithfulness** is three axes and recall is one of them, so it is not a promise of complete recall: each **holder** answers for its own boundary - today the coordinator alone, answering from the skeleton and the store, which it queries rather than holds resident (`0044`). The obligation that follows is on the negative answer - it states the boundary it is speaking for, because a bare *it is not known* leaves the reader unable to tell **not held** from **not found**, and deciding between those two is exactly the burden the system exists to remove. `0022` is the same rule at the write end: an empty extraction is reported rather than passed over in silence.

## Consequences

**This is not the completeness assertion `0002` forbids, and the line is where the two touch.** `0002` bars a **standing status** - *I am complete, I am fresh* - because a self-report that can go stale lies confidently, and it says in the same breath that it is not a rule against speaking. A **negative answer to one question, at the moment it is answered**, is the other thing, and it is required rather than merely permitted.

**A limit travels with this record.** Naming the boundary gets a reader from *it is not known* to *not in the skeleton and not in the store*. Ring 0 is not one of the things named: it governs **residency, not readability**, and is not a third persisted thing. It does **not** distinguish **nowhere** from **elsewhere**, because nothing today tells the coordinator that a second holder exists. #66 carries that residue and wakes when a holder exists that holds a fact the coordinator cannot see.
