# The system does not fetch; the boundary starts at the endpoint, and the endpoint is multimodal

The system does not fetch anything - Billy is the fetcher, and it does not need to know how a source arrived. The boundary starting at the endpoint moves the trust requirement from coverage of the world to **completeness of recall over what it was given**. Because delivery is a paste, the endpoint is multimodal from the first version, and it records the source's **publication** time and not its arrival time - three notices dumped on a Sunday in the wrong order would otherwise let an older fact silently overwrite a newer one.
