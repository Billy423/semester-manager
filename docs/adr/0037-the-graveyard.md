# The graveyard: removed fields, their reasons, and a standing rule against re-adding them

The field-set record lists **sixteen** removals with the reason for each, under a standing rule: *deliberately absent - do not re-add without a new ruling; a later session reading an older document must not restore them.* **The rulings bind; the numbers do not** - every count in the table was stated over the 22-row fixture or the two-course corpus, both since superseded, so a later reader must apply the removals and must never re-derive them from the arithmetic (ADR `0079` is the general form of that caveat).

## The table

Twenty rows: the sixteen at source, four of them re-reasoned and three flagged, plus four additions.

| # | absent thing | why it is absent, and its standing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `workload` · `hours_estimate` · `workload-estimate` (three names, one field) | The world does not supply it, it is not a unit anyone thinks in, and its null is not a gap. Size, where it matters, is **observed rather than stored** - ordinally, from `parts` and item notes first, then by asking for a relative comparison. Adversarial correction, attached the same day and part of the ruling: **asking is only a remedy for a quantity the user can answer**, and the answers available are ordinal comparisons, not hour counts |
| 2 | `status.completion` · `files` · `score` · `evaluation` | None of these is the system's burden. **This does not contradict the finding that a three-axis status prevented two live items being erased; it moots it, since nothing is asserted** |
| 3 | `count{done, of}` | **Reason replaced.** It counts attendance-as-score, which row 2 already covers. The original *"one instance in 22"* is dropped: it quotes a count this same table invalidated |
| 4 | `stated_in` · `source_ref` | **Flagged - the only row whose why-column is bare.** The source record says "not carried" and gives no reason; the no-re-add rule binds it regardless. ADR `0057` is the constraint that keeps it out through the annotation: `origin` may say **how** a claim came to exist and never **where in the source** it came from |
| 5 | `obligation.notes` | Under the **non-overlap rule** (ADR `0024`): a **negative** definition - *everything no mechanism reads* - cannot be non-overlapping, and across the fixture it carried **six unlike purposes**. All free text lives on annotations, which carry `created_at`/`updated_at` and a maintenance-at-read rule. This row is why ADR `0028`'s one-free-text-field cap is tractable |
| 6 | release dates (*"Starts February 9"*) | Noise. Nobody cares when an assignment was released, and the system needs it even less |
| 7 | per-part weights and per-part scores | Modelling sub-items costs more complexity than it returns. **Measured and knowingly given up** - one course's A1 splits `5% / 2.5% / 2.5% / 5%` and A2 `3.5% / 3.5% / 5.5%`, and 6 of 9 assignments in another are two independently assessed parts. **`parts[]` therefore carries no status and no score of its own** |
| 8 | coarse dates (*"April 2026"*) | **Flagged.** A date that is not fixed is null - so the term's largest obligation stores a null `due`. Both readings are defensible and the extraction went the other way; the ruling stands |
| 9 | recurring / countable obligations (weekly labs, quizzes, tutorial participation) | **Reason replaced.** Keeping them out explicitly is preferred to the complexity of representing them. **The `n=1` must not be restated** - it is no longer n=1, three further instances exist. The ruling stands under the no-re-add rule, and whether to hold it, re-open it or defer is an open decision for Billy (it is the one that decides whether a 5%-of-grade required obligation stays invisible to ring 0) |
| 10 | `status.evaluation` | Reaffirmed against the challenge that *"what do I still owe attention to"* is a deterministic query. **That challenge's hidden premise is that unread feedback is worth attention, and the only authority on that says it is not.** The strongest-reasoned row in the table |
| 11 | `course.offering_term` · `course.prereq` | **Reason replaced - the row that most needs it.** Both stated reasons fail: *"null for both courses"* is falsified at source (an outline states a prerequisite), and *"a domain that does not exist"* is a fact about the old container. Replacement: **out for v1 because v1's boundary is coursework; deferred to v2** (`D6`) |
| 12 | `course.manifest` | Exactly redundant with the rows - one course lists 15 and has 15, another lists 7 and has 7. An obligation declared but unscheduled is a row with a null `due`. A measurement that still holds |
| 13 | `course` free-text field | **Reason replaced.** *"Nothing identifiable would go in it"* is too strong - instructor, term boundaries, units, antirequisites and the required textbook all do go in it. The ruling **routes them to annotations**; the reason as written does not. Where they land, and what holds term boundaries, is `D23` and an open decision for Billy |
| 14 | `term_start` | **Flagged as the row most likely to need re-opening.** Its whole reason is a count over the dead fixture (*"one week-relative obligation in 22"*), and term boundaries are load-bearing for the last-day-of-classes rule |
| 15 | `due_precision` as a separate flag | The distinction lives in `due`'s own type. Clean and self-evidencing |
| 16 | `time_point` | **A deferral, not a removal - its own text says so, and the no-re-add rule does not bind it.** The type is real (an exam sitting, a review session, a conference) and is separate from `obligation` because only obligations consume the weekly hours; its reader is the calendar projection. **It is out because the projection is, not because nothing reads it** - see `D4`. The row stays in the table: the graveyard is a standing anti-regression device, and thinning it is the move it exists to prevent |
| +1 | `present` · `external_ref` · `backing: referenced_only` | **Added.** Billy-ruled: an artifact needs no URL and no presence flag; absence is the absence of store content, read as a join. See ADR `0011` |
| +2 | `supersedes` (link kind) | **Added.** Cut on five agents over two courses with zero instances; every real revision replaced the file in place, so a `supersedes` link would mistype three shapes and hide a live document. See ADR `0014` |
| +3 | `weighting_scheme` | **Added.** The general form of conditional weighting - naming the alternatives with a derived weight - was rejected as over-built for one concrete calculation. See ADR `0032` |
| +4 | the `<course_id>-slug(name)` id scheme | **Added.** A killed scheme rather than a field, and the regression this material is likeliest to make: constructing an id is a bet on reproducing another writer's spelling. See ADR `0025` |

Two corrections that must not be re-inherited from the source record: the table has **sixteen** rows, not fifteen; and the derived figure *"thirteen of fifteen carry no changelog line"* is over the wrong denominator and is dropped rather than restated.
