## Scoring against the pre-registration

Of the seven predictions: P2 (`has-more` placed as a field), P3 (`0100`'s labelling contested), P5 (J5 not a must) and P7 (`course` and `progress` in ring 0 against `0019` and the `obligation` entry) were hit; P6 (`or` falsified) was partially hit through the routes-to-material case; P1 (optional crossing scoped over the target predicate) and P4 (#14's judgment sentence read as a contradiction) missed - P1 because the draft had already stated the scoping rule, P4 because both reviewers accepted the input-versus-judgment distinction. Per `research-method.md` the exercise is scored on unpredicted yield, and that yield is the whole trajectory table: every substantive reshaping - the start from `obligation`, the withdrawal of the term predicate, the demotion of the reachability constraint, the empty relation position, the repaired line, the size gap - came from a finding outside the prediction list.

**The method finding, stated once.** Rounds three to seven found the same move at four seams: a field set transferred from a render record - `0038`'s band, `0096`'s edge, `0096`'s example, `CONTEXT.md`'s line - onto a routing position that `0101`'s criterion was supposed to derive. Each round repaired one seam by adding a rule to the type apparatus, and the next round found the rule applied at one seam and not the next. The fault was in the repair method, not in any one rule: those rules are the query grammar, which #85's own method finding placed after this map, and this ticket does not own them. The eighth draft demoted them to a draft section (§5b) and ruled only what the ticket was given, after which the lens-1 count went to zero and stayed there and the lens-2 count fell to two single-cell findings per round. The owner named the pattern before the eighth draft, asking where the failure was after seven rounds; the answer was the reasoning move, repair-by-adding-a-rule, and it is the same move #85's own trajectory records against its third and fourth drafts.

## Two things a reader should know

**The reviewers were withheld the session's analysis, not the material** (`research-method.md`). What they lacked that the material could not supply were rulings the owner made in the session; each draft's second paragraph lists them, and each brief told the reviewers to treat them as ruled and report only a landed record contradicting one. Two such contradictions were reported and each led to a demotion the owner accepted: the reachability constraint (review 4) and size's classification as an observation on a member (review 10).

**Findings that changed a ruling were sent to the owner rather than repaired**, and he ruled each: the intent's start (review 2 → `obligation`, not `course`); the term predicate (review 4 → withdrawn, one term assumed in the width bet); the observation's record (review 4 → parked whole, no chosen location); the standing of the three-bucket evidence (review 1 → evidence, not a source); size's classification (review 10 → demoted from *observation on a member* to *shape open*, and again at review 14 to *the record is not exhaustive on the shape*); and the demotion of the grammar-level rules to §5b (after review 14). Everything else was repaired in the next draft.
