### 1. The test's formalisation - deliverable 1

`0101`'s test in its informal wording: *a form belongs in routing's domain if and only if some judgment the coordinator must make ranges over a set that cannot be produced in one return without it.* The first application exposed three questions that change verdicts. Each is answered here.

**(iii) A form is counted at the grain where removing it changes what a chain can produce.** This is the test's own criterion applied to its own unit: `0101` says the test *"admits by what a form lets a chain produce"*. Under it, `FILTER(P and Q)` is a spelling of `FILTER(P) · FILTER(Q)`, so `and` is not a form. `or` is a form: without it, `FILTER(P or Q)` is `START · FILTER(P) ∪ START · FILTER(Q)`, a union, which the vocabulary does not contain and which is two returns. `not` and `exists` are each a form: `exists` is what carries a join, and `not exists` is the only way a chain tests absence on one member (`0003`). §7's *connectives as one form* is replaced.

**(ii) Two chains produce the same set relative to a judgment.** Two chains produce the same set for a judgment if and only if they differ only at positions the judgment does not range over. Read absolutely over the path set, every distinct chain trivially produces a distinct set and question (i) loses its discriminating power; read absolutely over endpoints, `0101`'s ground for paths - *"a judgment across obligations is a judgment about their relations"* - is discarded. The relative reading keeps `0101`'s output type unchanged and makes each must-judgment declare what it ranges over, which is a column of §3's table.

**(i) *Without it* is read over the candidate forms minus the one under test, including inside `exists`.** §7's provisional reading is confirmed. The fixed point the body feared - that the set of forms is what the test generates - dissolves under (iii): two spellings of one production are one form, so a candidate can never be excluded by a spelling of itself. The concrete case #85 §7 named, #88's *covered* disposition admitting `nodes_without` as a form and thereby removing the ground under `exists`, does not arise: `nodes_without(K, L, D)` is `START(K) · FILTER(not exists(MOVE(L, D)))`, a spelling, and whether it is separately named at the surface is #88's and says nothing to the test.

**The test can now be run.** Its instrument standing is unchanged: a finding at #87 or #88 that it admits or excludes wrongly revises it.

### 2. The two type questions - ruled here

**A crossing may be optional.** A member whose crossing finds no relation is kept with an empty position, and the empty position names the relation it ranged over. Ground: *what each obligation requires you to know* is in `CONTEXT.md`'s opening sentence, so it is a must-judgment; a mandatory crossing returns only the obligations that require something, and the reader cannot tell *requires nothing recorded* from *out of range*, which is the silence `0081` forbids and the omission axis of **faithfulness** names; the complement fetched separately is two returns, which `0101`'s premise forbids. **A constraint on the grammar found here:** optionality scopes over the crossing together with any predicate on its target. `MOVE(about, pointed-by)` reaches both annotation kinds by `0012`'s signature (`annotation → any`); if a kind predicate is applied after an optional crossing, an obligation with a `sticky_note` and no `progress` record is kept by the crossing and dropped by the predicate, and the optionality has done nothing. The spelling is the grammar's; the type requirement is ruled: *an optional step is empty when nothing satisfies the step's whole selection*.

**A non-endpoint position carries its kind's line.** The same deciding fields an endpoint carries, one field set per kind (`0095`), no depth. Ground: `0039`'s symmetry is scoped to the set the judgment ranges over, and a judgment over paths ranges over every position, so a position carrying only an id would make *which concept* in job 2 cost one `look_at` per intermediate, which `0101`'s premise forbids; a position carrying a block would carry depth the discard (`0043`) and `0095` bar. Course's line is `id` `name` `term` (`CONTEXT.md`, *the line*); `concept`'s and `artifact`'s are the debt that entry records, and this ruling fixes the type and not their content.

### 4. What this makes ring 0 - the standing intent

**Ring 0 is one routing result held resident by policy (`0101`), and this is the result.** The standing intent is derived from what J0 and J1 range over, under `0101`'s clause that an unprompted coordinator needs somewhere to stand.

```
START(course) · MOVE(course, pointed-by)? · MOVE(about, pointed-by)[kind = progress]?
```

A three-position path: **course → obligation → progress**, both crossings optional under §2's rule. Every course is a start; every obligation is reached by crossing its `course` field backwards (`0029` makes membership a Ref-typed field, which `0101` makes crossable); every obligation's current progress record is reached by crossing `about` backwards, at most one per target (`0035`). A course with no obligation keeps its path with position 2 empty; an obligation with no progress record keeps its path with position 3 empty, which `0035` reads as `not_started` and `0082` renders without an `id`.

**Why it starts from `course` and not from `obligation`.** `START(obligation)` makes a course with no obligation landed invisible, and the coordinator cannot then tell *this course has no obligation* from *this course is not in the system*. `0081` requires a negative answer to name its boundary; a resolve's boundary is its range, and a range over obligations cannot speak for a course. Starting from `course` makes each course speak for itself. This is the first case where §2's optional crossing is load-bearing rather than convenient.

**Why the predicate is empty.** J0 and J1 range over every obligation, dated or not (#14's bucket 2). No ruled source names a judgment that ranges over *the active ones* as a set: the opening sentence says *what is owed*, bucket 1 says *a dated sequence*, neither says *within a window*. Which obligations are near is a judgment the coordinator makes with the set, using `due`, `done_by` and `state` against today, and `0101` places a judgment with operations rather than in them. §7 says what this does to `0042`.

**Why it reaches `progress`.** `state` is J0's input (in-progress work is one of the three triggers Billy stated) and it is a field of `progress`, not of `obligation` (`0035`). `0046`'s rule that annotations arrive through their own channel is a delivery rule about a node's render and not a scope rule over routing's vocabulary (`reading-records.md`'s aspect table records that exact misreading); `about` is in `0012`'s table and crossable. The alternative - `state` copied onto the obligation's row - is what `0038` did, and it is the field-on-the-related-thing that `0016` bars; the path type makes it a position instead.

**What this changes in `0100`'s clause table.** `0100` placed `0042` as ring 0's **admission** clause, *which rows enter*. `0042` never excluded a row: every obligation enters, and the partition decides which rows carry four more fields. That is a **depth** partition, and `0100` half-saw it - *"`0042` does not itself say that ring 0 ranges over every obligation"* - without relabelling. Admission is the standing intent's range and predicate, ruled above; `0042`'s content is §7's.

**Width.** Five courses is roughly 55 obligations, a fall26 number whose evidence base excludes the obligation-dense course (`0038`'s scope clause); the standing result is one return of that width plus five course positions. `0101` leaves the width gate unruled, and this record does not rule it: holding every obligation resident is a bet on that figure, recorded as one. **What `refresh()` reports.** `0089` returns whole lines per changed obligation; under this record a change at position 3 - a progress record created or its state changed - is a change to the path, so it is reported as a change to that obligation's line. `0089`'s ground and its return shape are untouched; one pointer is owed.
