## Lenses

- **Lens 1 - attribution.** Does every cited record say what the draft attributes to it, for the aspect the draft uses it for (`docs/agents/reading-records.md`)? Does any landed record contradict a ruling in the draft that the amendments section does not already list for repair? Does the draft use a term `CONTEXT.md` avoids?
- **Lens 2 - falsification.** Find a judgment the coordinator must make, from a ruled source, that the must-list omits or that the standing intent cannot serve in one return; find a field the criterion admits that the line omits, or excludes that the line carries; find a chain under the draft's own type rulings (optional crossing, positions as lines) that silently drops a member; find any step that reasons from `0038`'s or `0042`'s present content back to the purpose; find any decision the draft makes that its closing section says it does not.

## Verdict thresholds

A finding is **blocking** if it (a) shows a cited record does not support the claim it is cited for and the claim is a ruling, or (b) exhibits a must-judgment from a ruled source that the standing intent plus the admitted forms cannot serve in one return, or (c) shows a ruling in the draft contradicting a landed record not listed for amendment, or (d) shows the standing intent silently dropping a member the must-list needs. A finding is **repair** if it changes an attribution, a wording or a table cell without changing any ruling. **Ambiguous resolves to blocking.** The draft posts only after every blocking finding is either resolved in the text or ruled on by Billy, and every repair finding is applied.

## Judgment calls, named up front
