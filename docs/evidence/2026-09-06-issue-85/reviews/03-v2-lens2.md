## Findings

1. **[Attack 1] §6 / §4 row 2 / §7 S5 - the admission predicate is not expressible in `P`, so ring 0's standing intent is not a chain.** §6: *"admission is today `0042`'s three triggers - expressible in §2's predicate language as two ranges and a disjunction."* `0042`'s third trigger is `state == in_progress`. `state` is not in `obligation`'s field set: `CONTEXT.md` (`progress`, `_Avoid_`): *"`obligation.status` is dead and `progress.state` is live"*, and *"A target with no progress record reads as `not_started`"* (`0035`'s default). §2 restricts `P` to *"a predicate over a node's own fields"*. The third trigger therefore needs a crossing (`START(progress) · FILTER(state = in_progress) · MOVE(about, points-at)`) and the union of that set with the two range sets, which §2 rules out (*"Set operations are not composition"*). No single chain of §2's steps produces band A in one return; the absence-as-default rule is carried by no atom. The row-2 cell *"two ranges and a disjunction"* also miscounts the triggers (two ranges, one equality, two disjunctions). The draft inherited `0038`/`0019`'s loose treatment of `state` as an obligation field without noticing that its own `P` makes the looseness bite. **blocking**

2. **[Attack 2, 3] §6 - `START(obligation) · FILTER(admission)` drops band B and contradicts `0042`, `0038` and `CONTEXT.md`.** `FILTER` *"keep[s] the members satisfying P"*; with `P` = `0042`'s triggers the result is band A only. `0042`: *"An undated obligation is in band B and that is not a hazard: it is present, it is routable"*; `0038` gives band B its own four-field row; `CONTEXT.md`: band A / band B are *"The two halves of ring 0's partition."* Ring 0 today is every obligation, and the triggers decide a row's band (its field set), not its membership. §4 row 1's own judgment, *"what is owed, across five courses"*, yields `START(obligation)` with no filter; the filter was placed because `0042` exists - the bottom-up move `0100` diagnoses. A second consequence: ring 0's rows carry two field sets by band, while §3 rules the output carries *"a per-kind field set"*; the draft's output type cannot represent the ring 0 it claims to be. **blocking**

3. **[Attack 4] §6 - *"refresh (`0089`) is re-running the standing intent"* contradicts `0089`.** `0089` rules that `refresh()` *"returns what changed in it"* as *"a whole line per changed obligation, plus an explicit marker for a row that is gone"*, and rejects the alternative: *"A full re-render on every refresh ... Rejected on that accumulation."* Re-running the standing intent is the rejected full re-render; producing a delta from a re-run requires the verb to remember what it last sent, which `0089` names as *"a second holder, whose cost is parked at #66."* The draft claims refresh is *"generated"* by routing and neither reproduces `0089`'s ground (addressing) nor its return. *"What changed"* is also not a chain over §2's vocabulary, which has no update timestamp (`0021`: no time axis). **blocking**

4. **[Attack 2] §4 premise - `0039` and `0043` do not support *"the unit is one return"*, and `0039` presupposes the opposite.** `0039` speaks about depth symmetry across members: *"observe(X) is permitted for a judgment over set S iff X is affordable for every member of S else dispatch(X, member) -> a value in the same shape as every other member's"* - one judgment served by one return per member. `0043` speaks about depth not coming back (*"rendered, used and dropped"*); it does not say the members of an earlier return are gone before the next return of the same judgment arrives. The inference *"a set that arrives across several calls is never observed as a set"* is the draft's, attributed to two records that speak about other aspects (the exact misreading `reading-records.md` tables). *"Billy's effectiveness constraint - the coordinator's call count may not grow with the graph"* is in no record; #84's raw thinking says only that the coordinator *"can find anything once it understands the need."* The one-call unit descends from #85's own agent draft (*"resident information plus one affordable call"*), not from the records. **blocking**

5. **[Attack 1] §1 - the spontaneity mechanism cannot form job 3's intent.** §1 rules *"the intent must be formable from what the current render exposes"* and *"The coordinator never needs the schema out of band."* S4's intent, `START(concept) · FILTER(has-no(covers, pointed-by))`, names `covers`, which `0092` says never appears in an obligation's `has-more` (`covers` is `artifact → concept`), and an absence cannot be read off a render that shows only presence. Ring 0 at spontaneity time exposes neither the kind `concept` nor the link kind `covers`; the tool description (#79) does. `0001`'s third job is precisely the intent this mechanism cannot form in-band. **blocking**

6. **[Attack 4] §8 (#86) decides #86 and forecloses the `0042` overturn #84 authorised.** *"size and absorption enter, if at all, as fields a row must carry for the spend-the-call decision, under §4's criterion."* #84 unfroze size and absorption *against `0042`'s admission clause*: *"The sentence most likely to move is the one below barring a notion of importance, because an obligation's size is one of the inputs that ruling was made without."* The draft confines both to selection, contradicting its own §6 (*"#86 supplying ... the admission predicate's content"*). **blocking**

7. **[Attack 4] §6, §3, §4 - selection and arrangement *"are the render's"* contradicts `0100`.** `0100`: *"Any clause's content, and ring 0's render, which is not one of the clauses. The render is derived from what the view holds and how it lays rows out"*; #84: *"The render is not one of ring 0's clauses."* The draft inverts the derivation - the clauses become parts of the render - and hands their content to #82, whose scope `0100` and #84 define as excluding clauses. **blocking**

8. **[Attack 4] §9.1 - the `0001` rewrite decides #86's territory and exceeds the map's authorization.** Job 2, *"manage cross-course information in the background"*, becomes *"cross-course relations are in the knowledge base's domain - modelled, not performed."* #84's starting input reads job 2 as the coordinator *"coordinating over five courses' per-course information"* and calls the two inputs *"scheduling's necessary inputs"*; `CONTEXT.md`'s `coordinator` *"writes the plan."* Ruling *modelled, not performed* pre-decides what #86 (and #14) may say the plan is. #84's overturn permission names `0042`, the plan and course information, not `0001`, and the draft rewrites the record the whole map's axis rests on (*"top-down from `0001`'s three jobs"*). **blocking**

9. **[Attack 4] §4 row 5 decides what §2 and §10 say is not decided.** Row 5 verdict: *"not a separate surface verb - `look_at`'s `<neighbours>` composes it (`0096`), and a second name for it fails `0024`."* §2: *"Not decided here: ... whether the two starts, or any step, are one surface verb or several."* #84 also warns: `0024` *"binds a verb once one exists ... invoking it against a capability would be the surface leaking down that `0070`'s test exists to catch"* - which is what the row does. **blocking**

10. **[Attack 2, 4] `REPEAT` is in §2's *"domain"* and §5's commitment, yet §4 admits it only *"when some judgment ranges over a closure - #88's finding"*, and §10 says that is undecided.** Either §2 admits a step before its own test does, or §2 is not the test's output as §0 claims (*"the necessity test that generates the read side's operation set"*). **blocking**

11. **[Attack 4] §7 S3 / §8 (#88) rules on `0089`'s sentence and rewrites #88's dispositions.** §8: *"`0089`'s 'the read side is two verbs' is audited by S3 ... so the sentence was true"*, and *"The disposition available is not covered / not covered but ..."*. #88's own text assigns *"revised at source"* to the covered case; the draft admits `START(K)` and `FILTER` into the domain (covered by its own test), yet §9 lists no amendment to `0089` and declares the sentence true instead. The sibling's frame is replaced from the frontier ticket. **blocking**

12. **[Attack 1] A cross-course judgment no chain serves: *which concepts do obligation A (2c03) and obligation B (2aa4) both require?*** Ground: `0001` job 2; `0009` (*"A concept appearing in two places is the truth of the data"*). `START(ref A) · MOVE(requires, points-at)` gives A's concepts; no atom of `P` names the other endpoint (`has(L, D)` carries kind and direction only), so *required by B* cannot be filtered, and intersection is excluded by §2 on vocabulary grounds (*"not in the vocabulary"*) rather than by §4's test, which admits by judgment. The draft's own claim that *"`has-no` covers the case difference would have served"* says nothing about intersection. Whether this judgment is a *must* is Billy's; the exclusion was never run through the test. **blocking**

13. **[Attack 1, 3] A `Ref`-typed field is in the vocabulary and no step follows it from a chained set.** `about` is `annotation → any` and a course is a node (`0008`), so *the obligations of the courses Billy annotated "dropped"* is `START(sticky_note) · FILTER(...) · MOVE(about, points-at)` to a course set, then nothing: `MOVE` crosses links only and `0029` makes membership a field. §2 says *"Nothing else in the vocabulary names a set"* after never deriving a step for `Ref`, though it says a Ref *"names"* a node. `0084`'s `obligations.list(course)` exists only on the fetch side. **blocking** (ambiguous whether the judgment is a *must*; resolved per the threshold)

14. **[Attack 2] `has-no(L, D)` row - *"without it"* is false under the draft's own `P`.** `P ::= ... | has(L, D) | ... | not P`, so `FILTER(not has(L, D))` produces the same set in one return, and §4's stated limit says *"Two spellings of the same set are one operation to it."* The verdict *"in"* claims more than the test supports; `has(L, D)` itself has no row at all. §9.1's *"derivation source of §2's `has-no` atom"* would need rewording (the set, not the atom). **repair**

15. **[Attack 2] `MOVE` with `Q` row - the ground contradicts §3.** *"after the crossing the link's fields are gone"* versus §3: a member reached by `MOVE` *"also carries the link it was crossed by - `type`, `direction`, and the link's fields."* The row still passes by extension (subset versus superset), but on a different ground, and *"in, when that judgment exists"* is not a verdict under an iff. - **Attack 4.** Quotations verified for `0046`, `0060`, `0092`, `0096`, `0098`, `0025`, `0017`, `0018`, `0029`, `0084`, `0089` (the id sentence), `0019`, `0021`, `0082` (*"transfer of the field set, not of a render"*), `0100` (*"what is undefined is routing"*), and `CONTEXT.md`'s `covers`, `applies` and `holder`; §9.2 and §9.5 stay inside the ticket; the HNSW verdict follows #84's own protocol for scaffolding; the fall26 caveat is present.
