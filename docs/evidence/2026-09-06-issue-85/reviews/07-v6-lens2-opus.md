I have read the draft, `CONTEXT.md`, all 34 named ADRs, the eight issues, and both standards docs. Findings below.

## Findings

**1. [Attack 1 — ruled types] §2's chain carries *members*; §3's output requires it to carry *paths*. The ruled input type is not well-defined.**
§2: *"An intent is a **chain**: a start, followed by steps, each of which either **filters** the current set by a predicate, **crosses** a relation... Chaining is one call: each step's output is the next step's input."* Every step is typed over members — *"filters the current set"*, *"A crossing from a member that has no such relation"*. But the same paragraph's predicate ranges over *"any earlier position on the path the member arrived by"*, and §3 rules *"Deduplication is by path"* and *"**A path is simple** - no node appears on it twice"*. Both require the pipeline to carry paths, not members: simple-path pruning is per-path state applied *during* the chain, and "the path the member arrived by" is not a function when a member is reached twice. §3 alternates in one sentence — *"A one-step chain's paths are the endpoints with the relation they were reached by; a start alone yields its **members**."* Counter-instance with divergent results: concept `c` reached from obligations A (course 2c03) and B (course 2aa4); under `START(obligation) · MOVE(requires) · FILTER(start.course = 2c03)`, a member-carrying chain keeps `c` once (some arriving path qualifies) and a path-carrying chain keeps one path and drops the other. That predicate is §7's first gap and, per §8, *"#86's first judgment to run"*, so the ambiguity lands on live work. `blocking`

**2. [Attack 1 — ruled types] The ruled type has no optional crossing and no union, so a judgment of the form "every member, with its X where it has one" cannot arrive in one return.**
§2 rules three step kinds and *"Set operations are not composition; they are not in the vocabulary"*; §2 also rules *"A crossing from a member that has no such relation yields no path from it."* Take the judgment named in `CONTEXT.md`'s opening sentence — the sentence §9.1 installs as `0001`'s whole job (*"Body: the job is `CONTEXT.md`'s opening sentence"*): *"what each obligation requires you to know, and where the material that teaches it lives."* `START(obligation) · MOVE(requires, points-at)` returns paths only for obligations that carry a `requires` link; obligations with none are absent from the return with no position of their own, so the coordinator cannot tell *this obligation requires nothing recorded* from *this obligation was not in range*. The word *each* is universal, and no chain expresses it: the two sets need a union, which §2 bars, and §4's premise bars assembling them across returns — *"Effectiveness alone would permit a union of k fixed chains in k calls; the ruling does not."* The same failure hits the third clause over `concept`/`covers`, where no resident set exists to subtract from (§6 holds one standing result and §10 leaves its range undecided). §3's mitigation covers only the wholly-empty case — *"An empty result names what it ranged over"* — so the per-member silence stands, against `CONTEXT.md`'s **faithfulness** (*"no relevant held fact is omitted"*) and `0081`'s *"a bare it is not known leaves the reader unable to tell **not held** from **not found**"*. Secondary: §2 cites `0046`'s *"where no edge exists the walk returns nothing"* for this semantics, but `0046` is a rule about a walk from **one** node, where silence is attributable to the node the caller named; over a set it is not. That is the aspect shift `reading-records.md` tabulates for `0046` itself. `blocking`

**3. [Attacks 3 and 4 — derivation direction / scope] §2 closes the step-kind taxonomy and rules `repeats` and Ref-typed-field crossings *into* the ruled type, which §7 marks conditional and §10 says the draft does not decide.**
§2: *"each of which **either** filters... **crosses** a relation the vocabulary names - a link kind in a direction, **or a Ref-typed field in a direction** - **or repeats** a crossing."* That is an exhaustive three-way, ruled. §7's table gives the same two forms conditional verdicts — `MOVE(field, D)`: *"in, if such a judgment is a must - #86's"*; `REPEAT`: *"in, if some judgment ranges over a closure - #88's"* — and §10 lists both as undecided: *"whether any judgment ranges over a closure... (#88)"* and *"whether `MOVE` with `Q` or `MOVE(field, D)` has a must-judgment... (#86)"*. If #88 rules that no judgment ranges over a closure, §4's test excludes the repeat and §2's ruled type is already wrong. The demotion of §7 is therefore nominal: §7's list is `FILTER / MOVE / REPEAT / exists`, and §2 rules exactly that enumeration one grain up. §7's own method finding — *"the enumerated list is the query grammar... and it cannot be completed before #86-#88 supply the judgments it must serve"* — applies to its closure into a type. This is `0100`'s diagnosis of `0038` at one remove (*"Filtering candidates against a criterion is construction"*), and §5 confirms where the enumeration came from rather than refuting it: *"§2's type maps onto a graph database's traversal model: a start is `V()`... a repeat is `.repeat()`, and the path output is `.path()`"* — a type read off an artifact is not made top-down by mapping it back onto the artifact. `blocking`

**4. [Attack 4 — attribution] §6's escape from `0043`/`0100` rests on a claim `0089` contradicts.**
§6: *"the standing intent is fixed, system-held, and **never issued by the coordinator**, and its result is resident by the policy `0019` names, **not by having been fetched**."* `0089` exists to supply exactly the opposite: *"A second verb, `refresh()`, refreshes the coordinator's resident view and returns what changed in it"*, and *"`0044` already states the behaviour without saying who triggers it... and **this record supplies the trigger**."* Under `0089` the coordinator issues the call and the result — whole lines per changed obligation — arrives by being fetched. §7's own S3 row says so: *"`refresh()` - one frozen resolve with no intent parameter."* The distinction §6 needs (intent not *formed* by the coordinator) is not the distinction the sentence makes, and it is the sole load-bearing step in threading `0100`'s *"`0043` bars a resident view from being a query."* `blocking`

**5. [Attack 2 — the test] The test's quantifier has no closed referent, so its verdicts are not stable, and its unit is undefined.**
§4: *"...no finite chain of **the other steps and forms** produces exactly in one return - where *other* excludes the one under test everywhere, inside a join included."* "The other steps and forms" presupposes the domain the test is supposed to generate, and §2 rules that set open: *"which starts, crossings and predicate forms the grammar carries is decided by the judgments #86-#88 supply, under §4's test."* The verdicts are therefore non-monotonic, and #88 has a live disposition that would flip one: #88 lists *"**Covered by the test** - it enters the capability list"* for `nodes_without(node_kind, link_kind, direction)`, whose set is `0003`'s *"nodes that have no link of the named kind in the named direction"* — precisely the set §7 admits `exists(chain)` on the ground that *"no other form tests absence on one member."* Admit `nodes_without` as a form and that ground is gone, with nothing in §4 stating a closure rule, an evaluation order, or that an "in" verdict is final. Second, the test's unit — *"A step or predicate form"* — is nowhere bound, and the granularity decides outcomes: `FILTER(P and Q)` and `FILTER(P) · FILTER(Q)` produce the identical path set, so `and` is redundant under the test and out, while `or` is not; §7's table nonetheless carries *"field comparison, connectives"* as one row, letting one form ride in on another. `blocking`

**6. [Attack 2 — the test] "produces **exactly**" is not bound to an extension, and §4's own two sentences need opposite bindings.**
§4's premise sentence: *"A judgment ranges over **a set**"* — a set of members. §3 rules the return is *"a set of paths."* The test compares the two without saying which is the extension. §4's stated limit needs both readings in consecutive sentences: *"Two spellings of the same set are one operation to it"* only bites if extension is the member set (two spellings differing in path structure would otherwise never collapse), while *"Under paths, a crossing and a join that tests the same crossing are **not** two spellings of one set: the first returns the relation, the second does not"* only holds if extension is the path set. Under the path reading almost nothing is ever redundant — any extra crossing changes the path set — and the "only if" half of the test admits nearly every proposed form; under the member reading the crossing/join distinction the limit rules collapses. The test cannot be run until this is fixed, and fixing it changes verdicts. `blocking`

**7. [Attack 1 — ruled types] §3 rules what an *endpoint* carries and leaves every non-endpoint position of a path unruled, at exactly the position §3's own justifying example depends on.**
§3: *"A path is the chain a member arrived by - node, relation, node, … - ending at an address, and **the endpoint** carries what decides whether it is worth the next call"*, and *"Every **endpoint** carries the same deciding fields and no depth."* Nothing rules what the intermediate `node` positions carry, and §10 does not list it. The example §3 uses to justify paths over endpoints is *"as paths it is *A - requires → c - requires ← B* and the judgment can be made"* — but §7 and §8 word that judgment as *"which obligations, across courses, require the same concept, **and which**"* (job 2). If `c` carries only an id, "and which" is unanswerable, by §3's own ground: *"An assigned id 'says nothing about the record it names'"*. Recovering it costs one `look_at` per intermediate concept — n calls growing with the graph — which is what §4 rules out as *"the coordinator's call count may not grow with the graph."* If `c` carries a line instead, that is a ruling §3 does not make and §10 does not reserve. `blocking`

**8. [Attack 3 — derivation direction] "Spontaneity" is presented as a restatement of something that exists nowhere, and §1's definition and §6's whole ring-0 conclusion rest on it.**
§1: *"**Spontaneity, restated as a mechanism.** Routing is not prompt-driven..."* — "restated" claims prior standing. The word and the claim appear in no ADR, in `CONTEXT.md`, or in the bodies or comments of #84, #85, #86, #87, #88, #16, #66, #82 or #12; the only corpus hits are in `research/`, which `CLAUDE.md` calls *"scaffolding: written once, never maintained"*, and they are about unprompted **speech** (#10's question), not routing. It is not among ¶2's five session rulings. It is load-bearing twice: §1 derives *"the intent must be formable from the vocabulary plus what the current render exposes"* from it, and §6's entire ground for ring 0's residency is *"Spontaneity needs somewhere to stand when nothing has been fetched."* The one plausible ground — `0001`'s third job, *"locate details Billy does not know to ask about"* — is the job §9.1 simultaneously demotes: *"not an objective to optimise."* `blocking`

**9. [Attack 4 — attribution/scope] §5's interim `applies` rule makes the system return a false negative that `0081` and §3's own empty-result clause forbid.**
§5, deviation table row 10: *"§9.5 proposes the entry's amendment, and **until it lands a resolve does not return paths over `applies`**."* `applies` is a link kind in `0012`'s closed set, so §2's ruled crossing type admits an intent over it. A resolve that crosses `applies` and returns nothing, while §3 requires that *"An empty result names what it ranged over - the kind, the source, **the relation**"*, tells the reader "no `applies` links in this range" when the links exist. That is the exact failure `0081` is written against — *"a bare *it is not known* leaves the reader unable to tell **not held** from **not found**, and deciding between those two is exactly the burden the system exists to remove"* — and `CONTEXT.md`'s **faithfulness** axis *"no relevant held fact is omitted"*. The alternative reading, that such an intent is refused rather than emptied, is not what the sentence says. `blocking`

**10. [Attack 4 — attribution] §2's address ruling and §9.5's `0089` amendment depend on an id-space question §2 itself says no record settles, and `0025` reads against it.**
§2: *"**An address** is a handle the query accepts: a node's `id`, and an edge's `id`"*, and §9.5: *"`0089`'s '`look_at(id)` reads one node' gains *or one edge*."* Two sentences later §2 lists as undecided *"whether an edge's `id` shares the node id space, which no record states"* (repeated in §10). `look_at(id)` takes one untyped parameter (`0093`: *"`id` | the read parameter"*), so it can dispatch node-vs-edge only if the two ids are drawn from one space — and `0025` scopes the one space to *"every kind that can be a link endpoint"*, which a link is not, while `0096` gives the edge's id a narrower stated purpose: *"Without it `attach` and `detach` have nothing to name."* The ruling is coherent only under a premise the draft declares open. `blocking`

**11. [Attack 4 — attribution] §1 calls the kind set closed and cites `0012` for it.**
§1: *"The vocabulary's closed sets - **the kinds**, the link kinds and their signatures (`0012`)."* `0012` closes the **link kind** set only. `CONTEXT.md`'s `kind` entry says *"**Current** set"*, and `0088` is built on kinds being added: *"Adding a kind adds a **render**, never a verb"*, with a consequence reserving *"A noun axis earns its place the first time a kind acquires an operation another kind lacks."* Fixing this changes no ruling in §1 — the out-of-band mechanism works with an open kind set — so `repair`

**12. [Attack 4 — attribution] §5's `0062` cell and §9.2's repair attribute chain-crossing of Ref-typed fields to an index that does not hold them.**
§5 row 7: *"files plus an adjacency index rebuilt at load | `0062`, repaired in §9"*, and §9.2 keeps `0062`'s conclusion *"on the claim, unmeasured, that an interpreter for a chain over **the adjacency index** is hand-writable at this size."* `0062`'s index is over links (*"all three graph operations are scans over that index"*), and §2 rules crossings include *"a Ref-typed field in a direction"* — in the reverse direction that is not an adjacency-index scan. The single landed instance is `0084`'s *"`obligations.list(course)` - a service read over an **indexed field**"*, one field on one kind. The claim is already flagged unmeasured, so the fix is a wording narrowing. `repair`

**13. [Attack 4 — attribution] §5 cites `0059` as if it already places a traversal that `0059` bounds to one hop.**
§5: *"`0059`'s persistence tier **already holds** *fetch-by-key and one-hop traversal*."* The quotation is exact, but the commitment it corroborates is a multi-hop chain returned in one call (§2: *"Chaining is one call"*), which is more than `0059` places there — and §10 reserves *"which tier holds routing's interpreter (`0070`)"*. Used as corroboration rather than as a ground, so `repair`

**14. [Attack 4 — attribution] §2 defines a layer as a kind, which `0027` and `CONTEXT.md` both bar in those words.**
§2: *"a **layer** is one of three kinds and names the same set as that kind today."* `0027`: *"**`layer` is a different axis** and only three kinds have one."* `CONTEXT.md`'s `layer` entry `_Avoid_` lists *"*layer* used for `kind`"*, and the `kind` entry avoids *"layer"*. The extensional claim is true today; the identification is the collapse both records rule against, and it silently licenses a predicate over `layer` as a field comparison when no record makes `layer` a field. Nothing in §1-§10 draws on `layer`, so `repair`

**15. [Attack 4 — scope] §9.5's amendment gives `look_at` an argument whose render no landed record can derive, and neither §9 nor §10 records the consequence.**
`0082` derives a render *"from its kind's field table"*; a `Link` is not a kind and has no field table — `0092` says so in passing: *"**`has-more` is in no field table**, and neither is `role`, which is a `Link` field."* `look_at(edge_id)` therefore has an undefined return, which is #12 item 4's territory (*"`look_at`'s return shape"*). Listing it in §10 changes no ruling. `repair`

**16. [Attack 4 — attribution] §1 cites `0096` for a fact `0094` states.**
§1: *"the one-hop neighbourhood inside every block (`0096`)."* `0096` rules how a neighbour arrives once there is a `<neighbours>` section (*"the edge gets its own element, and the neighbour's line sits inside it"*); the record that puts that section in the block is `0094` (*"Three positions fall out of that one sentence, and the render names them: the node's own fields, `<annotations>`, `<neighbours>`"*), with `0095` fixing its depth. `repair`

**17. [Attack 4 — attribution] §9.1 presents a non-verbatim string as a quotation of `0001`.**
§9.1: *"`0001`'s own ground - *"interpreting a notice, not reading it, forces a full reload"*"*. `0001` reads: *"it is *interpreting* a notice, not reading it, **that** forces a full reload."* Fair compression, wrong marks. `repair`

## What did not break the draft, per attack

**Attack 1 (ruled types).** I ran `0003`'s set (`START(K) · FILTER(not exists(MOVE(L, D)))`), S5's crossing-plus-negated-join set, `0013`'s closure read as endpoints, and `0017`'s two-locator duplicate-path case against the ruled types: all four are produced in one return, and simple paths preserve `CONTEXT.md`'s *"set of nodes reachable"* exactly, so §3's finiteness claim and its closure reading are sound.

**Attack 2 (the test).** I tried to break the one-return premise by finding a landed record that licenses assembling a set across returns — `0044`'s residency and `0089`'s refresh baseline were the candidates — and neither does: both keep a whole line set rather than joining fetched pieces, so the premise survives its grounds. The test also does generate `nodes_without`, which #85's body demands of it as a check.

**Attack 3 (derivation direction).** §3's paths-not-endpoints ground is genuinely top-down and survived: `0100` does ground the flat-render defect on `0001`'s second job in those words (*"which is a statement about relations across obligations rather than about any one of them"*), and §3 reasons from that judgment to the type rather than from any existing render. §6 also holds its line — it uses no clause content of `0038`, `0041`, `0042` or `0100` as a constraint on routing, and says so.

**Attack 4 (scope and attribution).** The `0029`/`0084` deviation row survived a hard check: `0084` does hold `0029`'s changed status and struck only the *"nobody walks it"* ground, so *"`0029`'s ground is untouched"* is exactly right. So did `0021`'s time-projection quote, `0019`'s two-persisted-things and *"an access policy"* claims, `0046`'s two quotations, `0017`'s locator-in-the-key consequence, `0097`'s addressability rule, `0100`'s *"`0043` bars a resident view from being a query"*, the `#17/#19/#20` line debt (which `CONTEXT.md` and `0095` both assign there), and every ticket assignment in §8 against #82's, #86's, #87's, #88's and #79's own bodies.
