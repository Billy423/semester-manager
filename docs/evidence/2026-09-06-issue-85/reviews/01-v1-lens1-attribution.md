**1. §6 (and §4, §8 #82) - "`0082`'s *obligation's line is ring 0's band* is not a field-set transfer; a ring 0 row **is** a line."** Cited: `0082`. The record says the opposite of what the draft attributes to it: *"obligation's line is ring 0's band by a **transfer of the field set, not of a render**... It says nothing about how a collection of obligations renders - ring 0 is not a sequence of lines by anything written here"*, and *"the transfer above stays sound for a **line** while saying nothing about ring 0's own."* `0095` repeats it (*"`0082` sets obligation's line by a **transfer**"*), `0089` leaves *"what element a ring 0 line uses is open"*, and `CONTEXT.md`'s `the line` places the line in exactly two places - *"inside an `<edge>`, and inside a composed section"* - neither of which is ring 0. `reading-records.md`'s aspect table names this precise misreading: `0082` read as *"a render identity, so ring 0 renders as lines"* when it binds *"a field-set transfer"*. The draft asserts the misreading as a ruling, builds §4's selection test on it (*"its rows are lines, so..."*), and tells #82 it is unblocked on it while claiming *"the render's shape is not touched here"* - but *a row is a line* is a render statement. §9 lists `CONTEXT.md`'s `the line` for a pointer repair, not for a third place, and does not list `0082`'s sentence at all. **blocking**

**2. §4 (absence row, and the `nodes_without` paragraph) and §9.1 - "`0081` requires the negative to be stateable"; "it fell out of `0081` rather than being placed"; job 3 *"survives only as what `0081` and the absence generator serve"*; `0003` *"re-grounded on `0081`"*.** Cited: `0081`. The record is a rule about the **form of a negative answer**: *"the negative answer states the boundary it is speaking for, because a bare it is not known leaves the reader unable to tell **not held** from **not found**."* It contains no requirement that a set-difference capability exist and nothing about links, layers or absence-of-a-link-kind. The record that derives `nodes_without` is `0003` - *"The third job is served by subtraction over the graph"* - from `0001`'s job 3, which #84 and #88 both record as *"top-down from `0001`'s job 3."* The draft moves the derivation from a record that states it to one that speaks about a neighbouring aspect (answer form, not capability), then uses that move to strike job 3 from `0001` and re-ground `0003`. **blocking**

**3. §2 ("`0046` routes it to dispatch"), §4 exclusion table ("who serves it: dispatch, by `0046`"), §8 #87 ("not in routing's domain by `0046`").** Cited: `0046`. The record bars the coordinator from searching - *"it does not search the corpus... the boundary is the tool surface, never self-restraint"*, *"a search is nearest-neighbour and is affordable once, not eight times"* - and never mentions dispatch. **4. §2 ("`0043` drops what is fetched, so the coordinator cannot hold an intermediate result between calls") and §4 clause (b) ("`0043` bars the coordinator from assembling by repeated calls").** Cited: `0043`. The record binds accumulation of **depth**: *"what is fetched is rendered, used and dropped, never accumulated... Holding expansions saves refetches and loses anyway."* It says nothing about intermediate results or repeated calls. The draft's own S1 cell concedes the gap: *"holding an id between calls is a handle, not depth."* A composed set's intermediate is a set of ids; under the draft's own reading of `0043`, holding those is permitted, and clause (b) - the clause that makes the transitive generator necessary and that the draft says is *"where this system differs from a graph database"* - has no record behind it. Ambiguous, and (b) is the load-bearing clause of the test. **blocking**

**5. §2 "Composition is one call... `0060` arrives at the same place from the grammar side."** Cited: `0060`. The record's mechanism is the opposite direction: *"each level renders what is around it and going one level deeper is one more call"*, and `0004` states the same rule as the size gate: *"one level deeper is one more call."* `0060` supports *one composable grammar*; it does not say a multi-hop composition resolves in one call, and read literally its progressive-disclosure clause is in tension with S5's *"filter ∘ one-hop ∘ absence, one call"*.
