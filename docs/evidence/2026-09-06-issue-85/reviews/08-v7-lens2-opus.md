**1. §3 — "Every endpoint carries the same deciding fields and no depth" is false across kinds, and the paragraph two lines down says so. — attack 4 — `repair`**

`0012` gives `about` the signature `annotation → any`, so a chain ruled legal by §2 ("a chain over the relations the vocabulary names - the link kinds in their directions") can end on endpoints of several kinds. `0095`: *"a line is **one** field set per kind and does not vary per row"* and *"**No rule generates a new kind's line**"*. §3's own next paragraph concedes the heterogeneity — *"for a `concept` or `artifact` endpoint the field set is the debt `CONTEXT.md`'s `the line` entry records against #17, #19 and #20"* — which cannot be true simultaneously with "every endpoint carries the same deciding fields". The load-bearing consequence is the following sentence, *"`0039`'s symmetry … is met by the output type"*: for a heterogeneous result the type does not meet it by itself, because two of the three reachable kinds have no field set at all. Correct wording is *each endpoint carries its own kind's deciding fields, at one depth* — which is what `0095` and `0097` (*"inside `<neighbours>`, which is heterogeneous, every row keeps it"*) already support, and which changes no ruling.

**2. §1 — `0092`'s `has-more` is cited as an in-band carrier that `0096` removed from a node's render. — attack 4 — `repair`**

§1, spontaneity: *"Which of it applies here reaches the coordinator in-band: `0098` spells names back, `0092`'s `has-more` lists a node's link kinds, `0096`'s `<edge>` carries `type` and `direction`."* `0096` rules the opposite for the aspect used: *"**So `has-more` has no place in a node's render at all.** … It survives **only** where the neighbourhood cannot be listed. That is ring 0"*, and `0092` agrees — *"what moved is where it appears"*, with the name travelling *"with **ring 0's own render**"*. Ring 0's render is undecided and is #82's, whose body says of `has-more`: *"`0092` rules what it carries; this rules whether and where it appears."* So the clause cites a render position that does not currently exist and leans on a #82 ruling not yet made. The spontaneity ruling stands on `0098` and `0096`'s `<edge>` without it, which is why this is repair and not blocking.

**3. §4 — the test is a success-path derivation, and `0071` is the record that says that provably misses a method. Neither §4 nor §10 mentions repair reads. — attack 4 — `repair`**

The test admits a form iff *"some judgment the coordinator must make ranges over a set that cannot be produced in one return without it."* `0071` (uncited anywhere in the draft): *"Two independent derivations of one method set … between them produced zero corrections, deletions, re-lands or crash paths, and the method that actually broke a system invariant came from neither"*, and *"Completeness over success paths is not completeness."* Concrete reads with no success-path judgment behind them: finding a dangling ref for `0018`'s *"link-set validation pass, which is **owed and unbuilt**"*, and finding the link a `detach` must name, given `0017`'s *"every change is detach plus attach"* and `0096`'s edge `id` existing precisely because *"without it `attach` and `detach` have nothing to name"*. §10 lists no repair-side residue. One line in §4 saying whether a repair judgment counts, or one line in §10, closes it.

**4. §9.1 — a new render criterion is asserted to preserve `0100`'s ground, in a record that §1 says makes no statement about renders. — attack 3/4 — `repair`**

§9.1 restates job 2 as *"cross-course relations are modelled by the knowledge base, and coordination over them is the coordinator's work (#14) - which keeps `0100`'s ground, since a render that hides a modelled relation is a defect."* No landed record states that criterion; `0082` states only its converse — *"The render may not invent a relationship the schema does not declare"* — and `0100`'s actual ground is the job-2 sentence itself, *"a statement about relations across obligations rather than about any one of them"*, which the restatement replaces. Meanwhile §1 rules routing is *"not a statement about renders"* and #82 owns ring 0's arrangement. Citing `0100`'s existing conclusion instead of minting a render principle here costs nothing.

**5. §8 — #88's disposition pair is narrowed, and the narrowing contradicts §8's own next sentence. — attack 4 — `repair`**

# Per attack: what I tried that did not break the draft

**1. The ruled kinds.** I built four candidate judgments and all four are servable under some precise type. (a) `CONTEXT.md`'s opening sentence read as one demand — *what is owed*, *what each obligation requires you to know*, *where the material that teaches it lives*, all without a reload — is one linear chain `obligation → concept → artifact`, and its two failure points (dropping obligations that require nothing; intermediates carrying only ids) are exactly the two §7 declares open, i.e. type failures, not kind failures. (b) The branching case — one judgment needing two different crossings from the same member, e.g. an obligation's `requires` concepts *and* its `spec role=owed` artifacts — genuinely cannot be a linear chain, and §2 bars the rescue (*"Set operations are not composition; they are not in the vocabulary"*); but every instance I could ground is either single-node (served by `look_at`'s block, `0094`/`0095`) or its second leg arrives as an endpoint field rather than a crossing, and asserting it is a *must* is #86's job, which the draft may defer.
