**How far the ADR count moved, and why it moved so little.** 84 → 80, a 4.8% reduction: two merges, two re-homings, no splits. The classified half moved 68 → 61 (10%) and called the group-and-merge hypothesis weakly supported. Across halves it is supported even less, and the reason is structural rather than surprising: **the two halves are largely disjoint in subject.** The classified half read the domain and spec records - the schema, the graph, the observation contract. The residue half read `evidence/` and `records/plan/` - method, derivation, acceptance, and how records bind. Decisions in one half rarely share a rejected alternative with decisions in the other because they are not about the same kind of thing. Where the two halves *did* touch the same object, the strict test cut both ways: `a-link-has-no-update` and `delete-does-not-cascade` merged cleanly, because each turned out to be a consequence of a classified ADR's own rejected alternative rather than a second decision; `park-with-a-wake-condition` and `under-model-deliberately` did not merge, though they are the same posture at two grains, because their reasons differ (epistemic against economic) and the merged body could not have fitted three sentences.

**Three items would have fallen between the two halves.** Each was routed *outward* by the classified half and *not received* by the residue half, so each was in neither set. ## `CONTEXT.md` - the final term set

**51 terms under 13 subheadings.** 46 from `10`, 3 from `11`, 1 from `12` (`the non-overlap rule`), and 1 promoted here (`closure`, whose ADR carries the trade-off and whose glossary line carries what it IS).

**The statement that belongs at the top of the file, unchanged from `10`:** these are the **engineering** names; ruling 9 says the product-facing verb names are undecided and obviously cannot be `ring 0` and `skeleton`.

### The grouping, and what changed

The scheme is `10`'s and it holds: **group by what kind of thing the term names, not by which record it came from.** A reader arrives holding a word and knows only its grammatical role. Twelve headings became thirteen:

1. **The two persisted things** (2)
2. **Nodes, links and pointers** (8) - **+1**, `closure`
3. **The two axes** (2)
4. **The kinds** (7)
5. **Fields that carried several names** (6)
6. **Two link kinds that need distinguishing by name** (2)
7. **The store's vocabulary** (4)
8. **The two written objects** (2)
9. **Residency and the read path** (5)
10. **The inbound path** (4) - **+1**, `candidate fact`
11. **Rules that are terms** (5) - **+1**, `the non-overlap rule`
12. **The project's own words** (2)
13. **Words for the project's own decisions and methods** (2) - **new**: `dissolved item`, `repair method`

**Why the thirteenth heading, stated so it can be overruled.** `dissolved item` and `repair method` are the residue half's two surviving coinages and neither fits the twelve. Both are terms the project made **about its own work** rather than about the domain: a `dissolved item` is a state of a parked question, a `repair method` is a class of operation identified by what no success-path derivation produces. Forcing `repair method` into group 9 would put a write-side method class among read-path operations; forcing `dissolved item` into group 12 would dilute the two words that carry the product's reason for existing. The alternative considered and rejected was dropping both to `docs/agents/` - rejected because `repair method` names something the system's method set must contain, which is a domain fact, not working discipline.

### The 46 from `10` - carried unchanged

All 46 terms, their definitions, their `_Avoid_` lines and the eleven `Dependencies satisfied` amendments carry verbatim. **Full text: `research/10-reconciled-classification.md` §The term set.** The writer transcribes that section, then adds the five entries below and re-groups per the list above.

The `grade_share` entry already names `worth_percent` under `_Avoid_`, which is the correct disposal: it tells a reader the two names denote one field, which is exactly the fact a reader of the substituted quotation cannot recover.

## `docs/adr/` - the numbered set

**80 ADRs, `0001` to `0080`, no gaps, no collisions.**

**The ordering principle.** Outward from why the system exists, through what it is made of, how it behaves, what it is built in and how it is built, ending with how the project's own records and measurements bind. `10`'s six groups are preserved as the spine of §1-§6 and are a good basis: a reader meets purpose before shape, shape before fields, fields before the contract that ranges over them, and the container last because it is the only group whose ADRs would change if the project moved off a plugin. Two sections are new, because the classified half never saw their material: **§7** holds the residue half's derivation and acceptance decisions, placed after the container because they are about producing the thing the reader has just finished meeting; **§8** holds the three ADRs about how documents and measurements bind, placed last because they tell the reader how to read everything above, including every `fall26` provenance citation in the repo.

**Reading the table.** *absorbs* names what the ADR carries. *full text* names the input file section a writer transcribes from; where a body is corrected here, the correction is stated in place and overrides the input.
